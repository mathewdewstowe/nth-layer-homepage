The Problem

Faster generation on a broken operating model produces more of the wrong thing, faster.

Most AI investment is running on the same decision structures, accountability gaps and measurement approaches that existed before the tools arrived. Teams are generating more output. Lead time to production has not moved. The spend line is rising and nobody can draw a line from it to a business result. That is not a tooling problem. It is an operating model problem.

What We Do

Three Ways to Engage

Every engagement starts with strategy: where is the constraint, what does rebuilding around it require, and what does success look like before any work begins. The shape depends on how urgent and how deep.

Diagnostic · 4 weeks

Find the Constraint

Map where the operating model is creating friction: where decisions slow, where value disappears and where the AI spend is going. You leave with a prioritised change plan and a clear read on where the value is.

Programme · 12 weeks

Rebuild Around It

Rebuild product strategy, delivery model and measurement together, not sequentially. Designed to show measurable results before the engagement ends. We transfer capability and do not create dependency.

Fractional · Ongoing

Senior Operating Model Leadership

Embedded in the business one or two days a week. Useful when the change is ongoing, the internal team does not yet have the pattern, and adding a full-time hire is not the right answer yet.

What Changes

The Four Numbers That Move

↓
Cost to Deliver a Verified Business Outcome
Total delivery spend against outputs that reached production and trace to a business result. Falls as decision quality improves and rework drops.
↓
Time from Decision to Measurable Result
Elapsed time between a priority being stated and its impact showing in operating data. Falls when the model is rebuilt around the real constraint.
↓
Dependency on External Capacity
Agency, contractor and consultant spend bought to compensate for gaps. Falls as throughput becomes predictable and capability is owned internally.
↑
Repeatability Across the Portfolio
When the approach runs more than once — across business units or portcos — the evidence from the first run calibrates the next. It compounds.
How We Know It Works

Falsifiable by Design

Before an engagement starts, we write down what failure looks like. If cost-per-outcome, lead time and change-failure rate have not moved by the end, we say so in writing — and we publish that finding. We have pre-registered that standard, not agreed it in retrospect. No engagement ends with a slide deck of claimed wins and no way to verify them.
