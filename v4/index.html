How it works

All Six Stages of the SDLC Rebuilt

01Before AI

PlanDesignBuildTestDeployMaintain

Coding was the slow step, so the process was built to ration it.

Illustrative proportions, not measured data.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Outcome

    Money Follows Evidence

    AI-assistedThe roadmap is fixed a year out and new ideas wait weeks for sign-off. Faster coding does not shorten time to market, so revenue from new features arrives no sooner.

    AI-nativeEvery idea is a hypothesis, tested cheaply with customers before real money is spent. Leaders fund the bets that show demand and stop the rest early, so more of the R&D budget turns into revenue.

  2. 02

    Design

    Outcome

    Risk Found While Cheap

    AI-assistedSecurity and compliance issues surface weeks later, when changing course is slow and expensive. Late fixes delay launches and add avoidable risk.76% find more compliance issues after release than during development.Source: GitLab, November 2025.

    AI-nativePolicy and compliance rules are written into the spec before building starts. Issues are caught while they are cheap to fix, so launches hold their dates and risk falls.

  3. 03

    Build

    Outcome

    More Output, Less Rework

    AI-assistedEach engineer codes faster, but still one task at a time. Mistakes surface at the end, so rework eats much of the gain and margins see little benefit.

    AI-nativeWork starts from a short written spec the team has agreed. Agents take routine tasks and each engineer runs several streams at once, so the same team delivers more without adding headcount.

  4. 04

    Test

    Outcome

    Everything Shipped Is Tested

    AI-assistedCode arrives faster than reviewers can check it. Work queues behind a few people or ships under-tested, and customers find the defects.

    AI-nativeEvery change runs its own tests before a person looks at it, and reviewers judge intent and risk, not every line. Quality holds as volume rises, so customers get more without more incidents.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Outcome

    Routine Changes Live Same Day

    AI-assistedFinished work waits for the weekly approval meeting. Small, safe changes queue behind large, risky ones, and value sits on the shelf instead of earning revenue.

    AI-nativeApproval scales with risk. Routine changes go live the same day and risky ones get a named approver, so customers see value sooner without extra risk.Moving from low to moderate automation lifts the chance of realising AI speed gains from 26% to 57%.Source: Harness, September 2025.

  6. 06

    Maintain

    Outcome

    Fewer Repeat Incidents

    AI-assistedIncidents are fixed when someone notices them. The lessons rarely reach the next plan, so the same problems return and keep costing customer trust.

    AI-nativeEach incident is traced quickly and turned into a permanent test. What was learned feeds the next plan, so repeat incidents fall and support costs fall with them.
