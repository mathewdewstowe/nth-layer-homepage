About this benchmark

The Nth Layer AI Maturity Benchmark measures how effectively PE-backed businesses are building, deploying, and scaling AI. Scores are based on 30 diagnostic questions across six pillars, benchmarked against 54 PE-backed SaaS businesses assessed over the past 18 months. Every score is derived from management responses and publicly observable evidence — not self-reported maturity claims.

Disclaimer: Cohort company benchmarks are derived from publicly available sources including company websites, investor materials, and published documentation. Data reflects information observable at the time of assessment and may be incomplete, outdated, or subject to interpretation. It should not be relied upon as a definitive or current statement of any company's position.
